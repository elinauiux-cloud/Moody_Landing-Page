Features

Vibe refiner

Describe it. Refine it. Return to it.

Write the moment, then shape it with quick refinements. Every theme you've explored stays one tap away in Earlier themes, so you can go back to last night's mood instantly.

  • Natural-language prompts
  • One-tap refinement chips

AI voice search

Just say it out loud.

Tap the mic and talk. MOODY shows a live waveform and transcript while it listens, then answers with a ready-to-play mix that matches what you meant — not only what you said.

  • Live transcript
  • Understands references and comparisons
  • Instant mix results
Search & Trends

Classic search, smarter discovery.

Find artists, albums and songs the usual way — or type a sentence. A dedicated Trends tab shows what's rising right now, from charts to the moods people are describing most.

  • Songs, artists, albums, playlists
  • Sentence-based search
  • Live trending moods
Library & Music DNA

Folders show the artists inside them, collections feel like records on a shelf, and your favourite artists live front and centre. Your profile turns listening into a personal Music DNA.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What makes MOODY different?

Instead of searching for a song, you describe a moment — "sunrise drive out of the city" — and MOODY builds the soundtrack around it.

Can I still search for specific artists?

Yes. Classic search for songs, artists, albums and playlists sits right next to natural-language discovery.

How does voice search work?

Tap the mic, talk naturally, and watch the live transcript. MOODY understands comparisons like "like Bicep but slower."

Is there a light mode?

Yes — Daylight, a warm paper palette. Switch between Midnight and Daylight in Settings.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Of course. Cancel in one tap from your profile; you keep Pro until the end of the period.
